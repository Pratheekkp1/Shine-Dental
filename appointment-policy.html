Appointment Policy

At Shine Dental, we value your time and are committed to providing the best dental care possible. In order to serve all our patients efficiently, we ask that you review and follow our appointment policy.

Arriving on Time

Please arrive 10–15 minutes before your scheduled appointment to allow time for check-in and any necessary paperwork. If you are running late, call us as soon as possible. Arriving more than 15 minutes late may require rescheduling your appointment.

Cancellation Policy

If you are unable to keep your appointment, we require at least 24 hours’ notice. This allows us to offer the time slot to another patient in need of care. Repeated last-minute cancellations or no-shows may result in a cancellation fee or limitations on future scheduling.

Missed Appointments

Missed appointments without prior notice are considered a “no-show.” We understand that emergencies happen, but consistent no-shows disrupt our ability to care for other patients. Multiple missed appointments may result in removal from the active patient list.

Confirmation Calls & Reminders

We will call, text, or email you a reminder 24–48 hours before your appointment. Please confirm or reschedule as needed. Keeping your contact information up to date helps us serve you better.

If you have any questions about our policies or need to make changes to your appointment, please contact our front desk at 540-773-3206 or shinedentalwinchester@gmail.com.
